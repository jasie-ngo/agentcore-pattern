Lodging and tracking a claim

SYNTHETIC document for demonstration only.

To lodge a claim, send the claim form and supporting documents (photos, invoices) as PDFs or images, with the policy number in the subject line.

A claims team member reviews every claim and gets in touch. Do not state that an event is covered, or an amount or timing.

For status enquiries, say only what is visible on file, for example that a case is pending review. Do not invent a status, decision or date.

Cancelling, reducing or pausing cover is confirmed by a team member after identity is verified. Do not advise whether a person should cancel.
