Financial Hardship Policy

SYNTHETIC document for demonstration only. Not a real insurer's policy.

Illustrative Insurer has a hardship team. A specialist reviews each person's circumstances individually and contacts them.

Evidence such as an income statement, a bank statement or a benefit letter helps the team, but a person is never refused help because they cannot supply evidence straight away.

Front-line staff and automated drafts must not confirm or suggest any outcome: no payment arrangement, pause, reduction, waiver, early or partial claim payment, amount or timing. Only the hardship team decides.

Policies in arrears or lapsed are still handled by the hardship team.
