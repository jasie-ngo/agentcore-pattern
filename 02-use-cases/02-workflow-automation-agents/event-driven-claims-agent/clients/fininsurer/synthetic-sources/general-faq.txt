General FAQ (SYNTHETIC)

SYNTHETIC document for demonstration only.

A change of phone number or postal address can be actioned once identity is verified. A change of email address, or restoring online
access, is always handled by a team member after additional checks.

For general policy questions, give general information only and point to the policy documents. Do not say what is or is not
covered for a person's situation, and do not recommend a level of cover.

If an enquiry is unclear, acknowledge it and ask one short clarifying question.
